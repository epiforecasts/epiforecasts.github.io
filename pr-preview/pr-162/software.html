R packages

R packages developed by the group

Reusable tools and packages developed by the group. We also write Julia packages. See also our ongoing projects and publications.

Most packages are on CRAN. The rest can be installed from our r-universe:

install.packages(
  "packagename",
  repos = c("https://epiforecasts.r-universe.dev", getOption("repos"))
)
